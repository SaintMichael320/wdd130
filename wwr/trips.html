From serene family floats to heart-pounding extreme rapids, Exciting Expeditions offers unforgettable river adventures for every skill level. Featured Expeditions

Sunset Family Float

Difficulty: Class I-II | Ages 5+

Perfect for families and first-timers, this gentle 4-hour journey down the Lower Canyon combines light rapids with peaceful stretches of calm water. Kids will love spotting wildlife along the riverbanks while parents relax and take in the stunning sunset views. Our guides share fascinating stories about the local ecosystem and geology. Includes a riverside snack break with refreshments.

  • Duration: 4 hours
  • Distance: 8 miles
  • Best for: Families, beginners
  • Season: May - September

Wild River Challenge

Difficulty: Class III-IV | Ages 16+

Experience the thrill of big water on this full-day expedition through Thunder Canyon. Navigate powerful rapids with names like "The Gauntlet" and "Roller Coaster" while enjoying breathtaking canyon scenery. This intermediate to advanced trip includes 6-8 major rapids and requires good physical fitness. Take a break mid-trip for a riverside lunch prepared by our guides. Swimming ability is mandatory.

  • Duration: 8 hours (full day)
  • Distance: 15 miles
  • Best for: Adventure seekers, experienced rafters
  • Season: April - October

Wilderness Expedition

Difficulty: Class II-III | Ages 12+

Immerse yourself in nature with our signature 3-day, 2-night camping expedition through Remote River Canyon. This journey combines exciting rapids, pristine wilderness camping, and gourmet campfire meals. Each day brings new adventures—from morning wildlife viewing to afternoon rapids to evening storytelling under the stars. All camping equipment, meals, and gear provided. This trip creates bonds and memories that last a lifetime.

  • Duration: 3 days / 2 nights
  • Distance: 35 miles
  • Best for: Groups, corporate teams, adventurers
  • Season: June - August

Extreme Rapids Experience

Difficulty: Class IV-V | Ages 18+

Only for the bold! This expert-level half-day trip tackles the most challenging section of Devil's Canyon, featuring continuous Class IV rapids and the infamous Class V "Dragon's Breath" drop. Previous rafting experience required. Our most experienced guides lead small groups of 6 rafters through heart-pounding whitewater. Sign a waiver, hold on tight, and prepare for the ride of your life. Not recommended for the faint of heart!

  • Duration: 5 hours
  • Distance: 6 miles
  • Best for: Expert rafters only
  • Season: May - July (high water)
